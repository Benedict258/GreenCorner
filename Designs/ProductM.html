About this board

A drop-in compatible board for learning and prototyping. It runs on the ATmega328P, works with the Arduino IDE and has the same pin layout as the original, so every tutorial, shield and sketch you find will work. A good first board for the smart dustbin, weather station and line-following robot projects.

Comes with a USB cable. Power it from a computer, a power bank or a 7 to 12V battery pack.
