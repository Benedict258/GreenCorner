About

Waste2Light Store is the components and kits branch of Waste2Light Ltd.

The same parts we use in our labs in Bosso, Minna, available to every student, school and maker.

Photo · the team with rechargeable lanterns · 5:4

Our story

Waste2Light started by turning discarded plastic bottles into rechargeable lanterns for students who study without steady power.

From there we grew into Green Energy Corners in schools, STEM kits for young innovators, and a locally built 800W wind turbine prototype. This store brings the parts behind all of it to anyone who wants to build.
