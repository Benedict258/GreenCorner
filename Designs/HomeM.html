Lighting tomorrow with today's waste.

Waste2Light started by turning discarded plastic bottles into rechargeable lanterns. Today we build renewable-energy systems, run Green Energy Corners in schools and train young innovators in Minna. This store is our components and kits branch: the same parts we use in our labs, available to every student, school and maker.
